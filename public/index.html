Hire a LEGO® setup expert

A flat $50 for a one-hour, on-site building session, plus a transparent travel surcharge.

  1. Pick a one-hour slot — available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
  2. Tell us your address. We calculate the travel surcharge from a live rideshare estimate (Civic Center → your location); if it can't be auto-estimated, we confirm the exact fare with you before any charge.
  3. Pay the $50 fee + travel surcharge securely. Your request goes to our experts.
  4. An expert accepts and arrives at your slot. Afterward, you're invited to leave a review.

The travel surcharge reflects the real cost of getting an expert to you and is shown as a separate line item at checkout before you pay.

Refunds & cancellations

  • Brick orders: unopened brick orders may be refunded in full within 14 days of delivery. Email us to arrange a return.
  • Setup bookings: cancel at least 2 hours before your slot for a full refund. Cancellations inside 2 hours are refunded minus the travel surcharge if an expert is already en route.
  • No expert available: if no expert accepts your booking, you are refunded in full automatically.
  • Refunds are issued to your original payment method via Stripe and typically settle within 5–10 business days.

Fulfillment

Brick orders are delivered or shipped within San Francisco. Setup bookings are fulfilled in person at the address you provide, during the one-hour slot you select.
