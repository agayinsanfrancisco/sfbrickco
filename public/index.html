02 / Service

Hire a builder

A flat $50 for a one-hour, on-site building session, plus transparent travel.

  1. Pick a slot. One-hour sessions, bookable within the next 24 hours.
  2. Give your address. Your request goes out to our builders — no charge yet.
  3. A builder accepts. Travel is priced from that builder's location to you (a live rideshare estimate) and added to the $50 fee.
  4. Pay & build. You get a payment link, pay in crypto, and your builder arrives at the slot. Afterward, you're invited to leave a review.

04 / The fine print

Policies

Please read before purchasing. These govern all orders and bookings.

Refunds & cancellations
  • Parts: unused parts may be refunded within 14 days of delivery — contact us to arrange a return.
  • Build sessions: cancel at least 2 hours before your slot for a full refund. Inside 2 hours, the travel portion is non-refundable if your builder is already en route.
  • No builder available: you only pay after a builder accepts, so an unaccepted request is never charged.
  • Refunds are sent on-chain to an address you provide; network (miner) fees may be deducted.
Fulfillment
Parts are couriered within San Francisco; the delivery fee is shown at checkout. Build sessions are fulfilled in person at the address you provide, during the one-hour slot you select.
Privacy
We collect only what's needed to fulfill your order: your Telegram handle, name, the delivery/service address you give us, a phone number for the courier, and your order history. Payments are made directly on-chain — we never see or store card details. We don't sell your data. To request deletion, contact us below.
